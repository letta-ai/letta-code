# Example prompts

Strong text-to-video prompts from model pages on fal.ai, quoted as published. Each link is the page the prompt came from. Use them as models of the level of detail that works: subject, materials, light, camera, lens, palette, timing, sound, on-screen text, and what to exclude.

## Single-shot still life: no people, light-driven

Source: [Seedance 2.5 text-to-video on fal](https://fal.ai/models/bytedance/seedance-2.5/us/text-to-video)

```text
Editorial still life for a luxury perfume brand, no people or hands in frame. A single heavy-based glass bottle with a faceted amber-tinted body and a brushed brass cap, standing on a slab of honed travertine against a warm sand-toned plaster wall. Beside it, a low-profile companion object for scale — a folded cream linen card embossed with the brand mark and a single dried sprig of eucalyptus resting on the stone. A slow drip of liquid catches the light on the bottle's shoulder. Hard, directional late-afternoon sunlight from the right rakes across the scene, casting a long, sharp bottle shadow and a bright caustic refraction on the wall behind it; a soft secondary bounce lifts the shadow side. Palette of amber, ivory, warm grey, and dull gold. Eye-level camera, slight three-quarter angle to the bottle, generous negative space above and to the left for type, medium-format look, 90mm lens, shallow but controlled depth of field, subtle film grain. Quiet, architectural, restrained, tactile, with the light doing the storytelling
```

## Kinetic typography with timestamped sections

Source: [H3 Max Turbo text-to-video on fal](https://fal.ai/models/minimax/h3-max-turbo/text-to-video)

```text
0.00–2.50
Warm ivory background.
Large black text: **H3 MAX TURBO.**
Text slides in fast from the left with a clean ease-out. A thin red line grows underneath it. On the beat, the whole composition compresses slightly and snaps back.

2.50–5.50
The red line extends across the screen and becomes a crop transition.
Reveal huge stacked text:

**FASTER**
**THAN REAL TIME.**

Use aggressive kinetic typography: large scale, edge cropping, fast horizontal movement, strong black/ivory/red composition. Keep the text sharp when it lands.

STYLE
Premium 2D motion design with very subtle 2.5D card depth. Swiss editorial typography mixed with energetic commercial motion graphics. Warm ivory, black and vermilion red. Large type, strong cropping, fast masks, cards, lines, parallax, precise easing, short motion blur only during transitions. No full 3D scenes, no UI, no particles, no random objects, no glitches, no letter-by-letter animation.

"fal" must always be lowercase.
```

## Period commercial with dialogue and an overlay

Source: [FLUX 3 Max text-to-video on fal](https://fal.ai/models/blackforestlabs/flux-3-max/text-to-video)

```text
1985 TV beer commercial, 35mm film grain, golden-hour sunset. One continuous slow push-in on a weathered cowboy leaning against a ranch porch rail, his horse tied up behind him. He pulls an ice-cold Lone Star from a cooler, cracks it open with a hiss, takes a long sip, and turns to camera with a tired grin. Warm amber tones, soft lens flare, slide guitar soundtrack.
Narrator (deep, gravelly): "Sunup to sundown, [pauses], he's earned every drop."
Cowboy (raising the bottle): "Now this is quittin' time."
Overlay: Lone Star logo fades in over the final frame.
```
